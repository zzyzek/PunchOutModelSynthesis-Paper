###### 2025-01-05

Some notes on submission to arXiv.org.

arXiv.org is pretty clear that papers shouldn't have conference or journal information unless
published, so I've stripped away the EXAG 2024 information for this submission and I'll
re-submit when the EXAG 2024 paper is published.

The conference proceedings should be submitted to [CEUR-WS.org](https://ceur-ws.org/)
(EXAG 2023 and before are there).
It looks like EXAG 2023 got submitted at the end of Januaray, so maybe we can expect
EXAG 2024 to be submitted then.
